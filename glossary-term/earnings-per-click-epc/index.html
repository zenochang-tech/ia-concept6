Earnings Per Click (EPC)

TL;DR

Earnings per click (EPC) is the average commission earned per click sent to an offer, calculated by dividing total commission by total clicks, and used to compare how well different offers or links perform.

What is earnings per click (EPC)?

Earnings per click (EPC) is the average commission a publisher earns for every click sent to an offer, calculated as total commission divided by total clicks.

Why publishers use it

EPC gives a single, comparable number across offers with different commission rates and prices.

  • High EPC — an offer converts well, pays well, or both.
  • Low EPC — worth checking whether it's the conversion rate (CVR) or the payout that's weak.

Why it matters

Because EPC blends both conversion performance and payout size into one figure, publishers often use it alongside click-through rate (CTR) to decide which offers are worth prioritising in their content or placements.
