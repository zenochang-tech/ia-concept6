## 2. Keep in English

These stay exactly as written, because Indonesian marketers use them in English or they're product names.

- **Brand and product names:** Involve Asia, Involve, Express Withdrawal, Membership Program, Bonus Campaign, Partner Discovery, Datafeed, Involve Asia app; all retailer and partner brands (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop…).
- **Roles (capitalised as in English):** Publisher, Advertiser, Account Manager, Creator (in compounds: "Partner Kreator" is used in the menu; see section 3).
- **Industry terms:** affiliate marketing, CPA, CPS, CPC, CPI, CPL, ROI, ROAS, KOL, API, SDK, postback, S2S, cookie, link (affiliate link), dashboard, offer, banner, voucher, cashback, conversion rate (in charts; see "konversi" in section 3).
- **Programme names on the dashboard:** Conversion Report → "Laporan Konversi" is translated (it matches the Indonesian dashboard); other menu paths in the app stay as the app shows them.
- **Tier names:** Silver, Gold, Platinum.
- **Currency/crypto:** USDT, TRC20, ERC20, stablecoin.

## 4. Numbers, money and dates

- **Thousands separator:** dot; **decimal:** comma. 1,500 → 1.500; 38.1% → 38,1%.
- **Rupiah:** "Rp" with no space: Rp15.000, Rp1.500.000. Short forms: Rp1,5 juta, Rp2 miliar.
- **Big numbers:** 1M+ → 1 juta+; 500+ → 500+.
- **Dates:** 12 November 2026 (day month year, Indonesian month names).
- **Prices quoted in RM or USD on English pages:** don't convert on our own. Each amount needs the Indonesian figure from the business (see section 5).
