## What is integration?

Integration is the connection between an [advertiser](advertiser)'s website or app, where conversions happen, and an affiliate platform like Involve Asia, where those conversions are tracked, attributed and paid.

When a shopper clicks a [publisher](publisher)'s [affiliate link](affiliate-link) and completes a qualifying action, the integration captures that event and sends it back to the platform, so the right publisher gets credit and the right commission is recorded.

## Who sets it up

Integration is the advertiser's responsibility. The advertiser adds the tracking code or connection, for example [pixel tracking](pixel-tracking), a [postback URL](postback-url) or an [SDK](sdk-software-development-kit) in an app. Publishers don't set anything up, but they depend on it working.

## Why it matters

- **For advertisers** — a correct integration records every sale, lead, install or action a publisher drives: no missed commissions, no double counting, no unattributed conversions.
- **For publishers** — every click and conversion you drive is recorded and credited to you. If the integration breaks, those commissions don't exist in the system, even when your traffic made the sale.
