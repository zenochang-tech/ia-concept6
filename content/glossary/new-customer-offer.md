## What is a new customer offer?

A new customer offer is built for people who haven't bought from a brand before. It can be a bigger discount, a welcome bonus, or a [commission rate](commission-rate) that is only paid when the resulting customer is genuinely new to the advertiser.

Advertisers often pay more to win a first-time buyer than a repeat purchase, because a new customer brings future revenue, not just one sale. That shows up as higher commission rates or stronger incentives than an [existing customer offer](existing-customer-offer).

## What to check

- **The T&Cs** — when an offer is limited to new customers, its [terms & conditions](terms-conditions-tcs) say so. Commission may only be paid if the purchase is confirmed as a first order.
- **Your audience** — content aimed at a broad audience will send some shoppers who already buy from the brand. Their purchases won't qualify under a new customer offer.
